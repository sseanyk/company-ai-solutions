Score every capture on board, then spend each ground pass on the images customers can actually use
Built for: Albedo (10 cm VLEO imagery)
Bottleneck: imaging satellites collect far more data than short ground-station passes can bring down. Albedo's X-band link runs at 800 Mbps, but a station sees the satellite for only about ten minutes, and very-high-resolution scenes are huge. Downlinking cloudy or low-value scenes wastes the pass.
